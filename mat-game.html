How It's Played

ONE MAT. FIVE ZONES. ENDLESS RALLIES.

Players sit or kneel around a cloth mat marked with numbered zones and aim tiles across it — each zone testing a different level of accuracy and nerve, from the safe Comfort Zone to the high-risk Banger Zone.

Built To Travel

NO COURT? NO PROBLEM.

Where Bomsy Wall Tennis needs a wall board and open space, the Mat Game rolls up and travels — making it easy to bring Bomsy play into classrooms, living rooms, and community halls. It carries its own tournaments and trophies, giving players a second way into the Bomsy family.
