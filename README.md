# Bomsy Games

Homepage concept for **Bomsy Games**, the Nigerian sports brand behind **Bomsy Wall Tennis** — a homegrown racquet sport played against a numbered target board, already contested in schools and universities across Rivers State.

## Contents

- `index.html` — single-file static homepage (hero, tournaments, gallery, news, footer). No build step; open directly in a browser or deploy as-is (e.g. GitHub Pages).

## Content sources

Copy is grounded in two sources:

- The Bomsy Wall Tennis Report (Issue #001, April 2025): the brand's stated vision ("bringing racket sport to your doorstep"), mission, and its tournaments — the Dietans International Schools BWT Tournament (Season 1), the 2025 Valentine Cup (Season 2) — plus the upcoming Rivers Secondary Schools Championship.
- [Vanguard News, "Bomsy Wall Tennis Births In Port Harcourt" (Sept 28, 2024)](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2024/09/bomsy-wall-tennis-births-in-port-harcourt/): the founder (Pastor Boma Willie-Pepple), the game's origin story, how it's played (racquet, tennis ball, numbered wall board, a target zone marked by two lines four feet apart, two 7-minute rounds), and its 2024 debut at Rivers State University of Science and Technology's Postgraduate Students Sports Week.
