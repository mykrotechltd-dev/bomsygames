BOMSY WALL TENNIS

Played with a racquet and a tennis ball against a wall-mounted board of numbered circles, players score by landing hits inside a target zone marked by two lines four feet apart — two rounds, seven minutes each.

Who We Are

A NIGERIAN-INVENTED SPORT, BUILT FOR THE NEXT GENERATION.

Bomsy Wall Tennis was born in Port Harcourt when founder Pastor Boma Willie-Pepple started hitting a tennis ball against a wall and, drawing on tennis and squash, decided to turn it into a game — developing it further with two tennis-playing friends before it debuted at Rivers State University of Science and Technology during Postgraduate Students Sports Week.

Vision

Bringing racket sport to your doorstep — making a homegrown discipline reachable in every school and every neighbourhood court.

Mission

To raise a generation of Bomsy Wall Tennis players at every level, and open an equal path to the global stage through an accessible, affordable racket sport.
