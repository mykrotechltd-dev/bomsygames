Who We Are

GAMES BUILT TO MOVE PEOPLE — LITERALLY AND SOCIALLY.

Bomsy Games is a growing family of innovative sports and recreational activities designed to make play more engaging, accessible, educational, and impactful.

Founded on the belief that games can transform lives, Bomsy Games continues to invent new ways to play — blending physical activity with simple, accessible tools so every game we build can reach more schools, more communities, and more players, wherever they are.
