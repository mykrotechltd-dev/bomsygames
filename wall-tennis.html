How It's Played

RACQUET. BALL. NUMBERED BOARD.

Played with a racquet and a tennis ball against a wall-mounted board of numbered circles, players score by landing hits inside a target zone marked by two lines four feet apart — two rounds, seven minutes each.

Singles format
Our Origin

BORN IN PORT HARCOURT.

Bomsy Wall Tennis was born when founder Pastor Boma Willie-Pepple started hitting a tennis ball against a wall and, drawing on tennis and squash, decided to turn it into a game — developing it further with two tennis-playing friends before it debuted at Rivers State University of Science and Technology during Postgraduate Students Sports Week.
